The exam

Capture, measure, score.

Three stages, in order. The clinician drives the protocol on one screen; the patient reads from a second. Stage three is not connected in this build, and the product says so.

01Working

Capture

Six guided tasks tell the patient what to say and for how long. A quality gate rejects a take that clips or sits too quiet to measure — while they are still in the room.

MPTPa-Ta-KaCAPE-VRainbowCoughSpontaneous
02Working

Measure

Acoustic features come straight off the take, computed in the browser — F0, HNR, jitter, shimmer, spectra. Measurements of the signal, never a diagnosis.

03Not connected

Score

The model reads those features and returns a risk score, written back to the chart as a note. Until a real model is behind it, the app refuses to show a number.

A placeholder score next to a real patient is a hazard, not a stub.

1A · motion spec
Device settle
On load, the recorder rises 24px and the drop-shadow deepens over 900ms, cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1). No bounce.
Callout draw
Leader lines draw outward (scaleX 0→1, 400ms, 120ms stagger) after the device settles; labels fade in behind them.
Live timecode
The on-screen timer counts and the waveform path redraws at ~12fps while the hero is in view — the only moving pixels above the fold.
Spec strip
Cells reveal on scroll left→right, 60ms apart, opacity + 8px rise only.
Spectrum bars
In the dark section, bars grow from baseline once, 500ms, staggered 18ms. They do not loop — it is a measurement, not a visualiser.
Gate state
“Take accepted” chip flips in from −4px with a 1-frame flash of the accent; “Model not connected” never animates.
Nav
Solid at rest; blurs and drops a hairline border past 80px scroll. Links get a 1px underline that grows from the left.
Reduced motion
All of the above collapses to a 200ms fade. Timecode freezes at 00:03:25.

Workflow

at the desk.

1B · motion spec
Light turns on
The radial glow behind the device fades 0→100% over 1.2s on load while the device scales 0.98→1. Feels like a lamp, not a slide.
Chips arrive as readings
The four measurement cards fade + rise 12px, 140ms apart, each with its number counting up over 600ms (F0 → HNR → Jitter → Gate). Gate lands last, in accent.
Baseline waveform
Hero waveform translates left continuously (24s linear loop) at 35% opacity. Amplitude grows slightly as the hero scrolls out.
Spectrogram paint
On scroll into view, columns reveal left→right over 900ms with a clip-path wipe, as if time were being written.
Workflow rail
Cards 01–04 lift 1px border to accent on hover; a hairline connector draws between them on first reveal.
Input rows
Status pills stagger in and briefly pulse. “Limited” and “Sandbox” pulse once in amber — honesty as a designed moment.
Nav
Transparent over the hero glow, becomes #0b0f14 with a hairline after 100px. The “Sandbox build” dot breathes at 2.6s.
Reduced motion
Everything static; counters show final values; waveform stops.
