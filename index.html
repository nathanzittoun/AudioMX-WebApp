Voice & speech biomarker platform

Capture a voice to protocol, measure it on the spot, and send the result back to the chart.

AudioMX pairs a purpose-built dual-MEMS microphone with a guided exam. The clinician drives the protocol on this screen; the patient reads from a second one. Every take is measured as it is captured, so an unusable recording is caught while the patient is still in the room.

Not connected USB-C on Chrome or Edge · Wi-Fi on localhost · computer mic anywhere

How a result is produced

Three stages, in order. The third one is not connected yet, and the app says so rather than showing a number.

  1. 01

    Capture

    The device streams audio to this page over USB-C or Wi-Fi. A guided protocol of six tasks tells the patient what to say and for how long, and a quality gate rejects a take that clips, sits too hot, or is too quiet to measure.

    Working
  2. 02

    Measure

    The browser computes the acoustic features from the take itself — F0, HNR, jitter, shimmer, spectra. These are signal measurements, not a diagnosis, and they are what the model will be fed.

    Working
  3. 03

    Score

    Dr Rameau's model is written in Python and will never run in a browser. It goes behind the backend, and its result is what gets written back into Epic as a note.

    Not connected

Before you rely on this build

Three things that are true today. The full list lives in LIMITATIONS.md.

Data stays in this browser

Patients, sessions and audio live in this browser profile only. There is no account and no sync: another computer, another browser, or cleared site data means starting from zero.

Epic is a sandbox

The chart connection reaches Epic's public test environment, not a real organisation. Use synthetic patients. No real patient data may enter this build.

No risk score is produced

Every number on screen is a measurement of the signal. The app deliberately refuses to show a score until a real model is behind it — a placeholder next to a patient is a hazard, not a stub.
